Gravitation Formulas

A quick reference cheat-sheet for Physics students

Newton's Law of Gravitation

F = G · (m1 · m2) / r2
  • F = Gravitational force between two masses
  • G = Universal gravitational constant
  • m1, m2 = Masses of the two objects
  • r = Distance between centers of the masses

Acceleration due to Gravity (g)

g = G · M / R2
  • g = Acceleration due to gravity on planet surface
  • M = Mass of the planet
  • R = Radius of the planet

Gravitational Potential Energy (U)

U = - G · M · m / r
  • U = Gravitational potential energy
  • M = Mass of the central body (e.g., Earth)
  • m = Mass of the orbiting object
  • r = Distance from the center

Escape Velocity (ve)

ve = √(2 · G · M / R)
  • ve = Minimum velocity needed to escape a planet
  • M = Mass of the planet
  • R = Radius of the planet

Orbital Velocity (vo)

vo = √(G · M / r)
  • vo = Velocity of a satellite in circular orbit
  • r = Radius of the orbit (R + altitude)

Important Constants

  • G (Universal Constant): 6.674 × 10-11 N·m2/kg2
  • g (Earth's Surface): ~9.81 m/s2
  • Mass of Earth (ME): 5.97 × 1024 kg
  • Radius of Earth (RE): 6,371 km
